# Candidate measurements for an external human COX-2 assessment

This is the historical candidate pool. A subsequently frozen, narrower set
plus seven independently sourced compounds was evaluated in the
[18 November external pilot](../cox2-external-pilot-2026-11-18/README.md).
That experiment failed; it activated no application model.

The table contains 62 original IC50 measurements for 60 standardized structures
on 34 nonchiral Murcko scaffolds absent from the complete previous 2,740-compound
ChEMBL panel. There are 24 active and 36 inactive compounds at IC50 <= 1,000 nM.
Repeated measurements retain their source IDs and count as one compound.

The planned external test requires at least 30 compounds per class. Current
support fails that requirement. These observations have not been used to fit,
select or evaluate an estimator. The table remains subject to protocol review,
including substrate differences, source unit/structure checks and patent-family
republication checks.

Only the listed assay descriptions were selected. The human enzyme is stated
in these descriptions or in the primary study for assay 2652_1. Multifragment
structures, nonexact/missing IC50 values, known shared publications and scaffolds
present in the earlier panel were excluded. No missing measurement was assigned
an inactive label. The CSV retains original SMILES, individual measurement IDs,
assay IDs, DOI or patent identifier, and record links.

The two additional patents US11478464B1 and US11786535B1 specify enzyme assay
conditions without identifying the experimental COX-2 species. Their six novel
compounds remain outside this subset.
